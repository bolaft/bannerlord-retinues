## Patch 32 (unreleased)

- Updated compatibility with Bannerlord 1.4.8 and fixed editor tooltips on the 1.5.4 beta.
- Fixed an error message when opening the clan screen on newer Bannerlord versions.
- Fixed a startup error from the Shokuho compatibility check when Shokuho is not installed.
- Fixed a cause of save crashes involving duplicate custom troops. Repairs preserve troop numbers, wounded troops and XP, and can also be run with `retinues.scrub_save`.
- Opening a hero's appearance editor now preserves their existing face.
- Crafted weapons remain available in the equipment list after reloading, including weapons made with smithing mods.
- Fixed the troop editor appearing upside down on some installations and total conversions.
- Fixed created, converted or imported troops spawning with missing or incorrect gear on Bannerlord 1.4+. For troops already affected, reapply their equipment in the editor or import them again.
- Retinues no longer appear as village volunteers or become available to AI parties through troop upgrades.
- Fixed custom militia not spawning in player settlements.
- Fixed kingdom settlements keeping vanilla recruits after the player inherits an existing kingdom.
- Custom troops copied from naval troop trees now retain their Mariner trait in-game.
- Fixed Cultural Pride notifications appearing when equipment costs are disabled.
- Fixed the Ironclad feat for kills by troops wearing tier 6 armour and helmets not progressing.
- Pragmatic Scavengers now requires two consecutive allied-army victories instead of three.
- Raised the maximum configurable skill total per tier to 9999. Default values are unchanged.
- A game update affecting one feature no longer disables unrelated features.
